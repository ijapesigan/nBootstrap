# nBootstrap: Nonparametric Bootstrap Confidence Intervals

Percentile, bias-corrected, and bias-corrected and accelerated
confidence intervals from a matrix of draws, and nonparametric bootstrap
and jackknife half-vectorized covariance matrices computed in C++.
[`PCCI()`](https://ijapesigan.github.io/nBootstrap/reference/PCCI.md),
[`BCCI()`](https://ijapesigan.github.io/nBootstrap/reference/BCCI.md),
and
[`BCaCI()`](https://ijapesigan.github.io/nBootstrap/reference/BCaCI.md)
are exported; the internal workers are copied by other projects.
